Close, rename and resize rmux sessions, windows and panes through the tmux tools: an rmux session in the Workspaces panel has the same pane Rename, divider arrows and Close, and Close session, each confirmed and sent as one exact-id command to the labelled rmux server. A pane title is sent as typed, because rmux does not expand formats in it as tmux does. No defaults change.
