Show Sessions, Jump To, the tab strip and the multiplexer gestures in local desktop sessions when the target finds tmux, Zellij or Herdr on its `PATH`: `local_capability` reports `tmux`, `herdr` and `zellij`, Sessions attaches a local multiplexer session as a new local terminal, and the saved workspace keeps the attachment. A local Herdr terminal stays a plain terminal until its tree and actions run on this device, and SSH sessions behave as before.
