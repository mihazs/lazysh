Close tabs from the native tab strip and rename Zellij tabs: a long press on a Herdr tab, a Zellij tab or a tmux window opens a Rename and Close menu, and Close asks first with a confirmation that names the tab. The only tab is never offered for closing. Changed default: a long press on a tab that can be renamed opens this menu instead of going straight to the rename prompt.
