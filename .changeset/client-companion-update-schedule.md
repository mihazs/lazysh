Show and change when the host companion updates itself, in Host settings. For a companion that has scheduled update checks, the **Companion updates** section now also offers which releases to follow (stable, or pre-releases too), how often to check (1 to 720 hours) and an optional window of the day for installs (the host's local time), turned off while updates are off, and says **Update available: version X** when the host's last check found one, or when it last checked. A companion without scheduled checks shows only the policy, as before.
