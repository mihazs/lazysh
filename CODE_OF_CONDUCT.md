# Community code of conduct

Everyone participating in lazysh issues, reviews and project spaces should be
able to collaborate without harassment. Be respectful, discuss evidence and
technical choices, accept constructive feedback, and credit others' work.
Welcome newcomers and allow time for people using a second language.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexualized conduct, publishing
private information, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Critique the
change or behavior, not a person's identity. Maintainers follow the same rules.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock a thread, or restrict
participation in proportion to the behavior. They should explain moderation
without repeating private information, and consider corrections and context.

For non-sensitive concerns, ask a maintainer for moderation on the relevant
thread. For sensitive conduct, use GitHub's **Report content** mechanism rather
than posting private evidence. A dedicated private project conduct channel has
not yet been established; see the [maintainer checklist](docs/community/maintainer-setup.md).
Do not use security advisories for unrelated conduct reports. No response-time
commitment is promised. This policy applies to project spaces and representation
of the project, including maintainers' conduct toward contributors.
